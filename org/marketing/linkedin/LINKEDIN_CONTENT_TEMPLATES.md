## 📋 Template Usage Guidelines

### Posting Best Practices
1. **Customize**: Always adapt templates to current events/trends
2. **Authentic Voice**: Maintain MantraPath's empathetic, professional tone
3. **Visual Elements**: Add relevant graphics/images when possible
4. **Hashtag Strategy**: Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post
5. **Engagement**: Respond to comments within 2-4 hours
6. **Timing**: Post during optimal engagement hours
7. **Value First**: Every post should provide value to the audience

### Content Approval Process
1. **Draft Creation**: Use template as starting point
2. **Fact Checking**: Verify all statistics and claims
3. **Tone Review**: Ensure empathetic, professional voice
4. **Legal Review**: Check medical/health claims (if applicable)
5. **Final Approval**: Team lead sign-off before posting

### Performance Tracking
- **Engagement Rate**: Target 5%+ for LinkedIn
- **Click-through Rate**: Target 2%+ for CTAs
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Monitor comment sentiment
- **Share Rate**: Track viral potential
- **Conversion Rate**: Measure beta signups, website visits

---

*These templates provide a foundation for consistent, engaging LinkedIn content. Adapt based on current events, platform trends, and audience feedback. Always prioritize authenticity and user value over promotional content.*
