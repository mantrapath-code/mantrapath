## 🚨 Crisis Communication Protocol

### Mental Health Emergencies
1. **Immediate Response** (0-5 minutes)
   - Acknowledge the person reaching out
   - Provide crisis resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
   - Move conversation to private if public
   - Alert internal crisis response team

2. **Follow-up Actions** (5-30 minutes)
   - Connect with appropriate resources
   - Coordinate with mental health professionals
   - Document incident appropriately
   - Plan ongoing support if appropriate

3. **Community Communication** (30+ minutes)
   - Address community concerns if public incident
   - Reinforce available resources
   - Emphasize community support guidelines
   - Provide additional mental health resources

### Brand Crisis Management
1. **Assessment Phase** (0-1 hour)
   - Gather all relevant information
   - Assess severity and potential impact
   - Consult with legal and leadership teams
   - Prepare initial response

2. **Response Phase** (1-4 hours)
   - Issue appropriate public response
   - Address stakeholder concerns
   - Implement corrective actions
   - Monitor ongoing situation

3. **Recovery Phase** (4+ hours)
   - Analyze lessons learned
   - Update crisis response procedures
   - Rebuild trust through consistent action
   - Prevent similar future incidents
