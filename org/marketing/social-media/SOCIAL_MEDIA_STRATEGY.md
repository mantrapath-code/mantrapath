## 🤝 Community Management Strategy

### Response Guidelines

#### Response Time Targets
- **Crisis Content**: Immediate (within 5 minutes)
- **Questions**: Within 2 hours during business hours
- **General Comments**: Within 4 hours during business hours
- **Direct Messages**: Within 1 hour during business hours
- **Weekend/After Hours**: Within 12 hours

#### Crisis Response Protocol
1. **Immediate Acknowledgment**: "Thank you for reaching out. Your wellbeing matters."
2. **Resource Provision**: Share crisis hotlines and immediate resources
3. **Professional Referral**: Encourage professional help when appropriate
4. **Follow-up**: Check back within 24-48 hours if appropriate
5. **Privacy Protection**: Move to DMs when necessary

#### Tone Guidelines
- **Empathetic**: Always lead with understanding and compassion
- **Professional**: Maintain clinical appropriateness without being cold
- **Hopeful**: Provide encouragement while being realistic
- **Inclusive**: Welcome all backgrounds, experiences, and identities
- **Boundary-aware**: Know when to refer to professional help

### Community Guidelines

#### What We Encourage
✅ Sharing personal experiences and insights
✅ Supporting others with empathy and kindness
✅ Asking questions about mental health and technology
✅ Providing resources and helpful information
✅ Celebrating progress and milestones
✅ Respectful disagreement and diverse perspectives

#### What We Don't Allow
❌ Specific medical or therapeutic advice
❌ Harassment, bullying, or discriminatory language
❌ Sharing personal information (doxxing)
❌ Promotional content unrelated to mental health
❌ Graphic descriptions of self-harm or suicide methods
❌ Conspiracy theories or misinformation

### Engagement Strategies

#### Proactive Engagement
- **Daily Monitoring**: Check mentions, tags, and relevant hashtags
- **Industry Participation**: Engage with mental health and tech content
- **Influencer Outreach**: Connect with mental health advocates
- **Community Events**: Host regular virtual events and discussions
- **User-Generated Content**: Encourage and feature community content

#### Reactive Engagement
- **Comment Responses**: Thoughtful replies to all relevant comments
- **Share Amplification**: Amplify positive mentions and shares
- **Crisis Support**: Immediate response to concerning content
- **Question Answering**: Comprehensive responses to user questions
- **Feedback Integration**: Acknowledge and act on user suggestions

## 🔄 Crisis Communication Plan

### Crisis Scenarios

#### Mental Health Emergencies
- **User posts suicidal content**
- **Someone reports self-harm**
- **Crisis in comments or DMs**
- **Community member needs immediate help**

#### Platform-Related Crises
- **Data privacy concerns**
- **AI malfunction or inappropriate responses**
- **Negative viral content**
- **Competitor attacks or false claims**

#### Company Crises
- **Security breaches**
- **Team member controversies**
- **Product failures or bugs**
- **Legal or regulatory issues**

### Response Framework

#### Immediate Response (0-30 minutes)
1. **Assess Severity**: Determine if life-threatening emergency
2. **Provide Resources**: Share crisis hotlines and immediate help
3. **Move to Private**: Take sensitive conversations to DMs
4. **Notify Team**: Alert crisis response team immediately
5. **Document**: Screenshot and record for follow-up

#### Short-term Response (30 minutes - 2 hours)
1. **Professional Assessment**: Consult with mental health professionals
2. **Resource Coordination**: Connect user with appropriate help
3. **Community Communication**: Address community concerns if public
4. **Platform Notification**: Report to platform if policy violation
5. **Follow-up Planning**: Determine appropriate ongoing support

#### Long-term Response (2+ hours)
1. **Policy Review**: Assess if guidelines need updating
2. **Team Training**: Address any knowledge gaps
3. **Community Education**: Share resources and prevention information
4. **Process Improvement**: Update crisis response procedures
5. **Stakeholder Communication**: Update leadership and partners

### Crisis Resources Database

#### National Crisis Resources
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741
- **SAMHSA Helpline**: 1-800-662-4357
- **National Alliance on Mental Illness**: 1-800-950-6264
- **Veterans Crisis Line**: 1-800-273-8255

#### Specialized Resources
- **LGBTQ+ Crisis Support**: Trevor Project (1-866-488-7386)
- **Teens/Young Adults**: Teen Line (310-855-4673)
- **Spanish Language**: Línea Nacional de Prevención del Suicidio (1-888-628-9454)

## 🎯 Influencer Partnership Strategy

### Influencer Tiers

#### Micro-Influencers (1K-100K followers)
**Target**: Mental health advocates, wellness coaches, lived experience shares
**Compensation**: Product access, small fees ($100-500)
**Content**: Authentic reviews, educational posts, community engagement

#### Mid-Tier Influencers (100K-1M followers)
**Target**: Licensed therapists, mental health professionals, wellness celebrities
**Compensation**: Moderate fees ($500-2000), partnership deals
**Content**: Expert endorsements, educational content, event participation

#### Macro-Influencers (1M+ followers)
**Target**: Celebrity mental health advocates, major wellness influencers
**Compensation**: Significant partnerships ($2000+), long-term contracts
**Content**: Major campaigns, event hosting, thought leadership

### Partnership Guidelines

#### Content Requirements
- **Authenticity**: Genuine experience with mental health or tech
- **Educational Value**: Must provide value beyond promotion
- **Brand Alignment**: Values consistent with MantraPath mission
- **Disclosure**: Clear FTC-compliant partnership disclosures
- **Community Benefit**: Focus on helping audience, not just promotion

#### Legal Considerations
- **Contracts**: Clear deliverables, timeline, compensation
- **FTC Compliance**: Proper disclosure requirements
- **Content Approval**: Review process for brand alignment
- **Usage Rights**: Clear guidelines for content repurposing
- **Termination Clauses**: Protection for both parties
