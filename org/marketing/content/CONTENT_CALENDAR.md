## 🚀 Content Production Workflow

### Planning Phase (Week 1 of month)
1. **Content Audit**: Review previous month performance
2. **Calendar Planning**: Next month topics and dates
3. **Keyword Research**: SEO opportunities
4. **Visual Planning**: Design asset requirements
5. **Team Assignments**: Writer, designer, reviewer roles

### Creation Phase (Weeks 2-3)
1. **Writing**: Blog posts, social copy
2. **Design**: Graphics, videos, infographics
3. **Review**: Content quality, brand alignment
4. **Optimization**: SEO, hashtags, CTAs
5. **Scheduling**: Platform-specific timing

### Distribution Phase (Week 4)
1. **Publishing**: Automated scheduling
2. **Monitoring**: Real-time engagement
3. **Community Management**: Response strategy
4. **Analytics**: Performance tracking
5. **Optimization**: Real-time adjustments

### Review Phase (End of month)
1. **Performance Analysis**: Metrics review
2. **Top Performers**: Content that worked
3. **Lessons Learned**: What to improve
4. **Strategy Updates**: Next month adjustments
5. **Team Feedback**: Process improvements

## 📈 Scaling Content Strategy

### Month 1-3: Foundation
- **Focus**: Brand awareness, education
- **Volume**: 15 pieces/week
- **Platforms**: LinkedIn, Blog
- **Team**: 1 content creator, 1 designer

### Month 4-6: Growth
- **Focus**: Community building, engagement
- **Volume**: 25 pieces/week
- **Platforms**: Add Instagram, Twitter
- **Team**: 2 content creators, 1 designer, 1 community manager

### Month 7-12: Scale
- **Focus**: Thought leadership, partnerships
- **Volume**: 35 pieces/week
- **Platforms**: Add YouTube, TikTok, podcasts
