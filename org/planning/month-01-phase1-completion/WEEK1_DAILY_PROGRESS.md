## 🧘 **Philosophy Review Framework**

### **Decision Checkpoint Questions**
Before each technical implementation, ask:

1. **Present-Moment Awareness**
   - Does this change promote user presence over distraction?
   - Will this feature guide users toward mindful engagement?

2. **Authentic Connection**
   - Does this enhance genuine human connection?
   - Are we facilitating vulnerability and authentic sharing?

3. **Single-Purpose Focus**
   - Does this maintain singular focus and prevent attention fragmentation?
   - Are we encouraging completion before progression?

4. **Universal Accessibility**
   - Is this accessible regardless of technical skill or economic status?
   - Does this serve inner peace for all users?

5. **Crisis Support Priority**
   - How does this impact users in emotional distress?
   - Are we prioritizing human connection over automation?
