**Philosophy-Aligned Decision Making**:
```

- [ ] I have clear guidance on what aligns with MantraPath philosophy and what violates it
- [ ] I can validate decisions against core principles before implementation

- [ ] I can integrate community wisdom into decision-making processes transparently
- [ ] I can ensure every decision serves user well-being over platform metrics or growth
```

**Decision Support Framework Features**:
```
Core Decision Categories:

├── Technology & Development Decisions:
│   ├── DO: Prioritize user well-being and spiritual growth in all feature development
│   ├── DO: Implement AI and ML to enhance human wisdom, never replace it
│   ├── DO: Design for contemplative, mindful usage patterns over engagement optimization
│   ├── DON'T: Add features that create dependency, comparison, or compulsive usage
│   ├── DON'T: Implement gamification or metrics that manipulate user behavior
│   └── DON'T: Compromise crisis support effectiveness for cost savings or convenience

├── Community & Moderation Decisions:
│   ├── DO: Foster authentic vulnerability and supportive community connection
│   ├── DO: Use restorative justice approaches that serve growth and healing
│   ├── DO: Celebrate diversity of spiritual traditions and healing approaches
│   ├── DON'T: Allow moderation to become punitive or shame-based
│   ├── DON'T: Create hierarchies that privilege certain users over others
│   └── DON'T: Permit platform features that enable spiritual bypassing or toxic positivity

├── Business & Sustainability Decisions:
│   ├── DO: Maintain free access to all core features serving inner peace
│   ├── DO: Optimize for long-term sustainability through efficiency and community support
│   ├── DO: Form partnerships with organizations that share philosophy and serve users
│   ├── DON'T: Introduce paid features that create artificial scarcity of wisdom
│   ├── DON'T: Accept partnerships that compromise user privacy or philosophical alignment
│   └── DON'T: Make decisions based on investor pressure or external growth expectations

├── Crisis Support & Safety Decisions:
│   ├── DO: Prioritize human intervention and dignity in all crisis situations
│   ├── DO: Integrate professional mental health resources with community peer support
│   ├── DO: Maintain 24/7 availability and cultural competency in crisis response
│   ├── DON'T: Rely solely on AI for crisis intervention without human backup
│   ├── DON'T: Compromise user privacy even during crisis situations
│   └── DON'T: Allow cost considerations to reduce crisis support quality or availability
```

**Decision Validation Process**:
```
Philosophy-Aligned Decision Framework:
- [ ] Automated philosophy alignment scoring for proposed decisions
- [ ] Community wisdom council review for major strategic decisions
- [ ] Historical decision pattern analysis to learn from past successes and mistakes
- [ ] Stakeholder impact assessment including vulnerable user populations
- [ ] Long-term sustainability evaluation ensuring decisions serve 5+ year vision
```
