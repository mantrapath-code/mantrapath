## 🌈 Visual Identity

### Logo System

#### Primary Logo
- **Format**: SVG, PNG, PDF versions
- **Minimum Size**: 24px height for digital, 0.5" for print
- **Clear Space**: 1.5x the height of the logo on all sides
- **Placement**: Prefer top-left or center positioning

#### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo**: Complete wordmark with icon
2. **Icon Only**: For social media profiles, favicons
3. **Wordmark Only**: Text-only version for headers
4. **Horizontal Layout**: Wide format for headers
5. **Stacked Layout**: Vertical format for square spaces

#### Logo Don'ts
❌ Don't stretch or distort the logo
❌ Don't change the colors
❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients, etc.)
❌ Don't place on low-contrast backgrounds
❌ Don't use outdated logo versions

## 💬 Voice and Tone

### Brand Voice Attributes

#### Empathetic
- **What it means**: Understanding and compassionate
- **How to apply**: Use inclusive language, acknowledge struggles
- **Example**: "We understand that mental health journeys are unique and challenging"

#### Professional
- **What it means**: Credible and trustworthy without being cold
- **How to apply**: Use clear, accurate language while remaining approachable
- **Example**: "Our AI technology is designed with clinical best practices in mind"

#### Hopeful
- **What it means**: Optimistic and encouraging
- **How to apply**: Focus on possibilities and positive outcomes
- **Example**: "Every step forward, no matter how small, is progress worth celebrating"

#### Inclusive
- **What it means**: Welcoming to all backgrounds and experiences
- **How to apply**: Use person-first language, avoid assumptions
- **Example**: "Mental health affects all of us, regardless of background or experience"

#### Clear
- **What it means**: Easy to understand and jargon-free
- **How to apply**: Explain technical terms, use simple sentence structure
- **Example**: "Our AI processes your conversations locally, meaning your data stays on your device"

### Tone Variations by Context

#### Website/Marketing Content
- **Tone**: Confident, informative, welcoming
- **Style**: Clear explanations, benefit-focused, action-oriented
- **Example**: "Experience mental health support that puts your privacy first"

#### Social Media
- **Tone**: Conversational, supportive, engaging
- **Style**: Shorter sentences, emojis appropriate, community-focused
- **Example**: "Your mental health journey is uniquely yours 💙 How are you taking care of yourself today?"

#### Crisis/Support Content
- **Tone**: Calm, reassuring, directive
- **Style**: Clear instructions, immediate resources, non-judgmental
- **Example**: "If you're in crisis, you're not alone. Here are immediate resources that can help"

#### Technical/Educational Content
- **Tone**: Informative, accessible, trustworthy
- **Style**: Step-by-step explanations, examples, clear benefits
- **Example**: "Local AI processing means your conversations with our chatbot never leave your device"

#### Error Messages/Feedback
- **Tone**: Helpful, apologetic, solution-focused
- **Style**: Acknowledge the issue, provide clear next steps
- **Example**: "We're sorry something went wrong. Here's how to fix it, and we're here if you need help"

### Language Guidelines

#### Words We Use
- **Mental health** (not "mental illness" unless clinical context)
- **Person with depression** (not "depressed person")
- **Healing** and "recovery" (emphasizing journey, not endpoint)
- **Support** and "resources" (empowering language)
- **Privacy** and "security" (emphasizing protection)
- **Community** and "connection" (highlighting belonging)

#### Words We Avoid
- **Crazy**, **insane**, **psycho** (stigmatizing language)
- **Suffer from** (prefer "living with" or "experiencing")
- **Normal** vs. **abnormal** (judgmental language)
- **Fix** or **cure** (mental health is ongoing, not a problem to solve)
- **Compliance** (prefer "engagement" or "participation")

#### Inclusive Language Guidelines
- Use gender-neutral language when appropriate
- Person-first language for mental health conditions
- Avoid assumptions about family structures, relationships, or economic status
- Include diverse perspectives and experiences
- Be mindful of cultural differences in mental health approaches

## 📱 Digital Applications

### Website Design

#### Layout Principles
- **Whitespace**: Generous spacing for readability and calm
- **Grid**: 12-column grid system for consistency
- **Alignment**: Left-aligned text, centered elements for balance
- **Hierarchy**: Clear visual hierarchy with typography and color
- **Navigation**: Simple, intuitive navigation structure

#### Component Guidelines
- **Buttons**: Rounded corners (8px), clear CTAs, hover states
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows, rounded corners (12px), organized content
- **Forms**: Clear labels, helpful error messages, accessible inputs
- **Navigation**: Consistent placement, clear active states
- **Alerts**: Color-coded by type, dismissible, clear actions

### Mobile Applications

#### Design Principles
- **Touch Targets**: Minimum 44px for accessibility
- **Scrolling**: Natural, responsive scrolling behavior
- **Loading**: Clear loading states and progress indicators
- **Feedback**: Immediate visual feedback for user actions
- **Accessibility**: VoiceOver/TalkBack support, high contrast options

#### UI Patterns
- **Bottom Navigation**: Primary navigation for core features
- **Floating Action Button**: Primary action when appropriate
- **Pull to Refresh**: Standard gesture for content updates
- **Swipe Actions**: Intuitive gesture-based interactions
- **Modal Dialogs**: Clear purpose, easy dismissal

### Social Media

#### Profile Setup
- **Profile Image**: Logo icon version, consistent across platforms
- **Cover Image**: Brand colors, key messaging, platform-specific sizing
- **Bio**: Clear value proposition, relevant keywords, link to website
- **Highlights**: Organized story highlights with branded covers

#### Post Design
- **Aspect Ratios**: Platform-optimized (1:1, 16:9, 9:16)
- **Text Overlay**: High contrast, readable fonts, brand colors
- **Watermark**: Subtle logo placement, doesn't interfere with content
- **Templates**: Consistent design system across posts
- **Accessibility**: Alt text for images, high contrast colors
