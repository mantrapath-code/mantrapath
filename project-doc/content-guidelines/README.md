## 📝 Content Guidelines by Platform

### **LinkedIn**
- **Length**: 1,300-2,000 characters for posts, 3,000+ for articles
- **Tone**: Professional, authoritative, engaging
- **Visuals**: High-quality images, infographics, technical diagrams
- **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant hashtags maximum
- **Timing**: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 1-3 PM

### **Technical Blogs**
- **Length**: 1,500-3,000 words for comprehensive guides
- **Structure**: Clear headings, code examples, visual aids
- **SEO**: Target keywords, meta descriptions, internal linking
- **Format**: Tutorial, case study, or technical deep-dive
- **Publishing**: Weekly or bi-weekly schedule

### **Case Studies**
- **Length**: 2,000-4,000 words
- **Structure**: Problem, solution, results, lessons learned
- **Data**: Quantifiable metrics and outcomes
- **Visuals**: Charts, graphs, before/after comparisons
- **Format**: PDF download with lead capture
