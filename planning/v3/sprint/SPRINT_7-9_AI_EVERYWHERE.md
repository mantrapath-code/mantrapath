# Sprint 7-9 Planning: V3 AI Everywhere & Advanced Voice Features
*Weeks 7-9: AI Intelligence Across All Services & Advanced Conversational AI*

## V3 Phase Overview
This phase focuses on implementing AI intelligence across every service and creating advanced conversational AI capabilities. We're transforming from isolated AI features to an AI-everywhere ecosystem where intelligence enhances every user interaction, with sophisticated voice conversations that feel natural and supportive.

### **V3 Phase Objectives**
1. **AI Everywhere Integration**: Extend AI intelligence beyond chatbot to all platform services
2. **Advanced Conversational AI**: Sophisticated dialogue management with emotional understanding
3. **Predictive Mental Wellness**: AI-driven early intervention and wellness prediction
4. **Cross-Service AI Learning**: Shared intelligence that improves across all user interactions
5. **Contextual AI Assistance**: Proactive, context-aware AI support throughout user journey
