## 📚 **CONTENT RESPONSIBILITIES & SCOPE**

### **Primary Content Areas**

#### **1. Mental Health Education Articles**
- **Anxiety & Depression**: Understanding, coping strategies, professional guidance
- **Mindfulness & Meditation**: Practical techniques, guided practices, benefits
- **Stress Management**: Work-life balance, relaxation techniques, boundary setting
- **Self-Care & Wellness**: Daily practices, lifestyle changes, holistic approaches
- **Crisis Support**: Emergency resources, intervention guidance, safety planning

#### **2. Affirmations & Positive Psychology**
- **Mood-Specific Affirmations**: Tailored to anxiety, stress, sadness, confidence
- **Daily Motivation**: Morning routines, evening reflections, weekly themes
- **Self-Compassion**: Building self-esteem, overcoming self-criticism
- **Resilience Building**: Growth mindset, overcoming challenges, personal strength

#### **3. Interactive Content**
- **Guided Exercises**: Breathing techniques, meditation scripts, journaling prompts
- **Wellness Challenges**: 30-day programs, weekly goals, community activities
- **Assessment Tools**: Self-reflection questions, mood check-ins, progress tracking

#### **4. Cultural & Linguistic Adaptation**
- **Regional Sensitivity**: Cultural norms, religious considerations, local resources
- **Language Variations**: Idioms, expressions, cultural references
- **Inclusive Content**: Diverse perspectives, accessibility considerations

### **Content Quality Standards**

#### **Mental Health Accuracy**
- **Evidence-Based**: Cite research, studies, and professional guidelines
- **Professional Boundaries**: Clear disclaimers about professional medical advice
- **Safety First**: Crisis resources, emergency contacts, professional referrals
- **Trigger Warnings**: Content notes for sensitive topics

#### **Writing Style & Tone**
- **Compassionate**: Warm, understanding, non-judgmental
- **Accessible**: Clear language, avoid jargon, multiple reading levels
- **Actionable**: Practical steps, concrete examples, measurable goals
- **Inclusive**: Diverse perspectives, cultural sensitivity, accessibility

#### **Technical Requirements**
- **SEO Optimization**: Mental health keywords, search-friendly titles
- **Structured Content**: Clear headings, bullet points, scannable format
- **Multimedia Integration**: Images, videos, audio, interactive elements
- **Mobile-First**: Responsive design, touch-friendly, quick loading
