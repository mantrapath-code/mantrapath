# Sprint 10-12 Planning: V3 Platform Optimization & Launch Preparation
*Weeks 10-12: Performance Excellence & Global Launch Readiness*

## V3 Phase Overview
This final phase focuses on achieving production excellence for our voice-enabled, AI-everywhere mental wellness platform. We're ensuring optimal performance, comprehensive cultural validation, and global launch readiness with world-class user experience and platform reliability.

### **V3 Phase Objectives**
1. **Performance Excellence**: Achieve sub-300ms response times for voice interactions across all features
2. **Global Cultural Validation**: Complete multi-cultural testing and cultural intelligence validation
3. **Production Readiness**: Deploy robust monitoring, security, and scalability infrastructure
4. **User Experience Perfection**: Polish all interactions for natural, intuitive user journeys
5. **Launch Ecosystem**: Complete documentation, onboarding, and support systems
