### Strengths
1. **Strong AI Integration**: All services have excellent AI capabilities
2. **Cultural Intelligence**: Comprehensive cultural adaptation across services
3. **Real-time Processing**: Good event-driven architecture
4. **Mental Health Focus**: Purpose-built for mental health applications
